Who We Are

OUR STORY

Born From The Streets

Drip Steps & Wordrobe started as a dream in 2026 — a vision to bring premium streetwear culture to Hathras at honest prices. We believe fashion is a form of self-expression, and every person deserves to express themselves with quality pieces that last.

Our collections are carefully curated, blending global streetwear aesthetics with an Indian sensibility. Each drop is limited, each design intentional. We don't do mass production — we do meaningful fashion.

From Instagram to your wardrobe, we're here to redefine what premium streetwear means in India. Simple orders, honest prices, real drip.

DELIVERY INFO

CASH ON DELIVERY

We only accept Cash on Delivery (COD). Pay when your order arrives at your doorstep. No online payment required. Simple and safe.

🚀

FAST SHIPPING

Orders are processed within 24–48 hours of confirmation. Standard delivery takes 4–7 business days across India.

HATHRAS ONLY

We currently deliver only within Hathras, Uttar Pradesh — 204101. Local delivery, fast and reliable.
